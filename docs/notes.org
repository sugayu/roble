* Speed of resampling
~roble~ implements several methods for resampling the spectral bins.
Please see [[file:../src/roble/resample.py]] for the details of the implementations.

Here this section put results of compulational speeds of the resampline methods.
The default method in ~roble~ is ~searchsorted~, the fastest among the all.
